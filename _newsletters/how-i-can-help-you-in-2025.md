As we close out 2024, here's a brief, self-serving update about how I can help you as we head into the new year.

**My goal is to make digital transformation easy for you** by focusing on how to maximize value without the buzzword nonsense.

**These are the five ways I deliver that:**

1. Newsletter
2. Quick Take
3. Strategy Session
4. Advisory
5. Intensives

[**Newsletter**](/newsletter) (free)  
This will continue to be sent every weekday with the occasional breaks for holidays and vacation.

If you find this valuable, I would love for you to tell someone else about it and have them [subscribe here](/subscribe-to-newsletter).

It's also shaped by feedback from readers like you, so if you haven't told me more about your motivations, [let me know here](/newsletter-subscriber-motivations).

[**Quick Take**](/quick-take) (free)  
This is a practically zero commitment way for you to get directional guidance on a challenge you're facing.

If you're interested, [use this form](/quick-take) to tell me what's on your mind, and I'll respond with a 5-minute personalized video with key questions to consider, potential paths to explore, and suggested next steps.

[**Strategy Session**](/strategy-session) (paid, $1k)  
This is a 90 minute session that dives into where you want your organization to be, what's working, what's not, and to define the path to get you there.

I recommend that you [start with the Quick Take](/quick-take) before scheduling this to ensure that I'm the right person to help you.

[**Advisory**](/advisory) (paid, $2k to $30k)  
This is a monthly service where I support your organization's digital transformation as an on-call advisor, and at the highest tier, to build solutions alongside your team.

[**Intensives**](/intensives) (paid, $3k to $60k)  
These are dedicated blocks of time in half day, full day, or full week increments to focus deeply on discovering, designing, and developing your organization's transformation. Advisory clients receive discounts based on tier and session type.

**Thank you for the opportunity to be in your inbox** each week.

**I look forward to growing with you in 2025** and hope that you have a fun, safe, and healthy celebration this evening.
