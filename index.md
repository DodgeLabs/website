## You’re not short on opportunities. You’re short on shipping change that sticks.
</header>

<div class="card__body" markdown="1">
If you’re the person who owns the outcome — COO, ops lead, advancement, partnership, or practice lead — you already know the pattern:

- The “temporary” process that became the real process
- Three systems that almost talk to each other
- A vendor or consultant who left you with something you can’t run
- A roadmap that grows while shipping stalls

I work with growth and mid-market operators who are done treating symptoms. You don’t need another deck. You need a clear path from messy reality to systems and habits your team can own.

**Right fit if:** you make the call (or sit next to the person who does), you want honest tradeoffs, and you’re ready to change how work gets done — not decorate the mess with another project.

**Wrong fit if:** you want bodies on a bench, a rubber stamp on a tool you already picked, or someone who will build around a broken process without saying so.
</div>
